ABOUT // JOURNEY SO FAR

MAKING THE
COMPLEX CLEAR

I’m a product designer drawn to complicated journeys, emerging technology, and the small moments that decide whether a product feels understandable.

THEN // NOW

I began with screens.
Curiosity pulled me
towards systems.

I started my career close to the interface — learning how layout, interaction, and visual hierarchy can make a product easier to use. Working across healthcare, mobility, and AI quickly showed me that the most interesting design problems often begin long before a screen is drawn.

Today, I work across research, product strategy, interaction design, and visual craft. I care about tracing a confusing moment back to its cause, giving teams a clearer direction, and turning that direction into something people can actually use.

I’m currently pursuing an MS in Human-Computer Interaction at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, adding deeper research practice and behavioural understanding to the experience I built in industry.
