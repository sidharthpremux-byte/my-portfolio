SELECTED PHOTOGRAPHY

THROUGH
THE LENS

Photography is where I practise attention without a brief — following light, structure, movement, and the quiet details that are easy to miss.

FIELD_NOTES // VISUAL PRACTICE
01 // ATTENTION

Looking before deciding.

Design often asks me to reduce ambiguity. Photography lets me stay with it a little longer — to notice what changes when the frame, distance, or light changes.

02 // LIGHT

Follow the light.

Shadows reveal form, depth, and timing before the subject does.

03 // STRUCTURE

Find the order.

Lines, repetition, and small breaks in a pattern turn ordinary places into compositions.

04 // MOVEMENT

Wait for the interruption.

A person entering a frame, a reflection shifting, or a vehicle passing can give a static scene its rhythm.

05 // ARCHIVE

Keep a visual memory.

The final gallery will mix architecture, street details, movement, and quieter observations from everyday life.
