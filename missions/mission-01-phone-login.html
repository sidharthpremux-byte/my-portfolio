01 // MISSION BRIEF

PROJECT CONTEXT

Eliminating onboarding friction for Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

Ather Energy's app adoption in Tier 2 and 3 cities was severely hampered by a mandatory email-based login system. In regions with lower email penetration, this single friction point was enough to stop users from ever accessing the product. The fix wasn't more onboarding — it was removing the barrier entirely.

02 // PRIMARY FLOW

THE HAPPY PATH

A streamlined OTP authentication journey with zero passwords.

The primary flow was designed to be effortless for users in Tier 2/3 cities. No passwords, no email, just a 10-digit number.

03 // THE ICEBERG

THE REALITY

What happens to 5 years of legacy email accounts?

03.1 // THE LOGIC

The Engineering Challenge

Instead of locking users out when multiple old emails were tied to the same phone number, we found a way to merge their accounts into one, so they didn't have to contact customer support.

Five years of email signups meant plenty of people had duplicate accounts tied to the same phone number. Rather than treating duplicates as a system error, we made account cleanup a simple, self-serve step right inside the login flow.

03.2 // THE RESOLUTION

KEEP ONE. SWEEP THE REST.

Whenever someone logged in and had multiple accounts attached to their phone number, we laid them out clearly on a single screen.

They tapped the account they actually use, and the system cleaned up the rest automatically. Simple, fast, and no support tickets required.

04 // REFLECTION

REFLECTION

I assumed people were comfortable logging in with an email. The PM's research showed otherwise, especially in Tier 2 and 3 cities, where many people got confused at the email step. That changed how I thought about the flow. The simpler it is, the less people have to think, and the more smoothly they get into the app.

Working with the back-end team taught me a lot too. I got a much better feel for tech constraints and what is feasible. I also saw how much easier everyone's work gets when the team talks early and often.
