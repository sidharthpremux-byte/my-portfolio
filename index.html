About

I didn't start with pixels.
I started with questions.

Over the last three years, I've designed products for mobility and healthcare—industries where confusion isn't just frustrating, it's a real barrier.

What keeps me going isn't polishing interfaces. It's understanding why people get stuck, then clearing the path forward.

Now I'm pursuing my Master's in Human–Computer Interaction at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, studying how to design systems that adapt to people, not the other way around.

Asking questions until the problem is clear
Removing steps instead of adding features
Turning research into decisions, not presentations
Designing systems, not just screens
Working with engineers without losing context
Prototyping fast to fail faster
